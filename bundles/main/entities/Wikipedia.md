# Overview

**Wikipedia** is a free-content, collaborative online encyclopedia written and
maintained by volunteers. It is published under a free license (CC BY-SA) and
is one of the largest and most-visited reference works on the internet. It is
owned by the Wikimedia Foundation.

# Role in This Wiki

Wikipedia serves as a source for this wiki's first ingested article: the entry
on [/concepts/Exempli gratia](/concepts/Exempli gratia.md) (e.g.). Wikipedia
articles are tertiary sources — they synthesize and summarize primary and
secondary sources — making them useful starting points for concept pages but
not authoritative primary references.

# Key Characteristics

- **Founded**: January 15, 2001, by Jimmy Wales and Larry Sanger
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA)
- **Languages**: 300+ language editions; English Wikipedia is the largest
- **Model**: Anyone can edit; changes are reviewed by volunteer editors and automated systems
